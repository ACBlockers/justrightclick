Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Just Right Click doesn't collect, transmit or sell any data. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.

What stays on your Mac

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information in its own storage on your Mac. None of it is sent anywhere.

  • Settings — which actions, templates and apps appear in the menu, and your menu bar and Enhanced Mode choices.
  • Templates — copies of the built-in blank documents and of any files you add as templates.
  • Folder access — the folder you choose when you grant access, saved by macOS as a security-scoped bookmark so the app can create files and open folders there.
  • Diagnostic logs — a short local record of actions and errors, which can include file and folder names and paths. Each log is limited to about 1 MB, and older entries are rotated out.
  • Last action — the result of your most recent action, shown in Settings.
  • Request markers — short-lived markers that stop an action from running twice. They are deleted after about 24 hours.

Permissions

  • Folder access (the folder you choose): used only to create the new files you ask for and to open folders in the apps you pick.
  • Accessibility (Enhanced Mode only, off by default): used only to recognize the Shift-right-click gesture. The app doesn't read or record what you type and doesn't send input to other apps.
  • Automation of Finder (Enhanced Mode only): used only to ask Finder for the current folder and the selected items.

Your files and clipboard

The app doesn't read the contents of your documents. It reads a template only to copy it into a new file, and it uses the paths of the folders and items you act on. Copy Path writes paths to the clipboard only when you choose it; the app never reads the clipboard.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send and use it to reply to you. The app's Feedback button opens your own mail app; nothing is sent automatically.

Deleting your data

Everything above stays until you remove it. To delete it, remove the app and its folders in ~/Library/Containers and ~/Library/Group Containers. You can turn off the Finder extension at any time in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
